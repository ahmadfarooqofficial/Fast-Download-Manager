Thank you for downloading FDM

Fast Download Manager is free, open source, and built for people who just want their downloads to be quick. Enjoy it.

Support the developer. FDM has no ads, no telemetry and no paid tier. If it saves you time, starring the project or telling someone about it is genuinely the whole reward.

Found a bug or have an idea? Issues and pull requests are welcome — it is your project as much as anyone's.

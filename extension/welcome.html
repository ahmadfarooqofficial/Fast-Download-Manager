What happens now

  • Click a link as usual

    Chrome hands the download straight to FDM. There is nothing new to click and no button to remember.

  • FDM splits the file

    It opens many connections at once and keeps re-splitting whichever part is slowest, so one bad connection cannot hold up the rest.

  • It lands in the right folder

    Files are sorted by type as they finish, so your Downloads folder stops being a pile.

Folders FDM will use

    Downloads FDM leaves alone

    Private-window downloads, and links that only exist inside a page (blob: and data:), stay with Chrome — a separate app cannot fetch those. You can turn takeover off at any time from the FDM button in the toolbar.
